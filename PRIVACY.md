# Privacy Policy — Playlist Desk

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Playlist Desk is a browser extension that lets you search, filter and edit your own
YouTube playlists.

**What it accesses.** When you open the panel on youtube.com, the extension reads the
playlists of the YouTube account you are signed into, using YouTube's own web
interface and your existing session. It only reads and edits playlists when you ask it
to (Sync, Remove, Move, Copy).

**Where data is stored.** The playlist index (video titles, channels, durations,
thumbnails URLs) is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB so searching is
instant. It never leaves your device.

**What it sends.** Requests go only to youtube.com, exactly as the YouTube website
itself does. The extension has no server of its own, no analytics, no crash
reporting, no accounts, and no third-party services.

**What it collects.** Nothing. The developer receives no data from users of this
extension.

**Removing your data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes its local storage.

**Contact.** https://x.com/SpartA1ah1 · https://github.com/1ah1/playlist-desk/issues

Playlist Desk is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
YouTube or Google.
